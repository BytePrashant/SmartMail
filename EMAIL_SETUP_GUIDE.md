# 📧 Gmail Setup Guide - SUPER SIMPLE!

This guide will help you set up your Gmail in SmartMail in just 2 easy steps!

## 🚀 **Setup in 2 Minutes!**

### **Step 1: Enter Your Gmail Address**
- Click "Setup Email" in SmartMail
- Type your Gmail address (like `john@gmail.com`)
- That's it for step 1! ✅

### **Step 2: Get Your Gmail App Password**
- **Enable 2-step verification** on your Google account
- **Generate an app password** (NOT your regular password!)
- Enter the app password in SmartMail
- Click "Test Connection" to make sure it works
- Click "Save & Use" to finish! ✅

## 🔐 **How to Get Your Gmail App Password:**

### **Step 1: Enable 2-Step Verification**
1. Go to [Google Account Security](https://myaccount.google.com/security)
2. Click on **"2-Step Verification"**
3. Click **"Get Started"**
4. Follow the setup process (usually involves your phone number)
5. **Turn it ON**

### **Step 2: Generate App Password**
1. Go to [App Passwords](https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords)
2. Click **"Select app"** → Choose **"Mail"**
3. Click **"Select device"** → Choose **"Other (Custom name)"**
4. Type a name like **"SmartMail"**
5. Click **"Generate"**
6. **Copy the 16-character password** (like: `abcd efgh ijkl mnop`)

### **Step 3: Use in SmartMail**
1. Paste the app password in the "App Password" field
2. Click **"Test Connection"** to verify it works
3. Click **"Save & Use"** to finish!

## ⚠️ **Important Security Notes**

- **NEVER use your regular Gmail password** in SmartMail
- **ALWAYS use the app password** you generated
- **Keep your app password private** - don't share it
- **App passwords are safe** - they only let SmartMail send emails

## 🎯 **That's It!**

Once you've completed these steps:
1. ✅ SmartMail will remember your Gmail settings
2. ✅ You can start sending emails immediately
3. ✅ All the technical stuff is handled automatically
4. ✅ You can change your email anytime by clicking "Change Email"
